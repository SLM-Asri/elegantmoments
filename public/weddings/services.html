Our services

Book one, or let us follow the whole story from pre-shoot to homecoming.

01 · Pre-shoot

Before the big day, a story of two

Pre-wedding couple photoshoots at lakesides, heritage buildings, temples, beaches or in the studio. Running barefoot through lakeside grass, dancing under an old stone arch, a spin beneath a lighthouse, a night drive in red. A pre-shoot lets you get comfortable in front of the camera, and tells the story of the two of you before the wedding.

Your wedding day, in its best light

Bride and groom portraits on the wedding day, for Kandyan and western weddings. A Kandyan groom and bride walking across the lawn. A ball gown and a long veil in the garden. Golden light on bridal jewellery. We take couple portraits on the day in the best light, honouring the traditions of your celebration.
