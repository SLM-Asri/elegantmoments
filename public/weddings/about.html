About Elegant Moments Weddings

Wedding photographers based in Anuradhapura, North Central Province, Sri Lanka.

Our story

A story you will want to open again and again

We don't just take photos on your wedding day. We follow the little looks, the nervous laughs and the happy tears, and turn them into a story you'll want to open again and again.

Our pre-shoots have taken couples to lakesides, heritage buildings, temples, beaches and our studio. Our galleries include Kandyan weddings, traditional white wedding attire and western white-gown weddings.
