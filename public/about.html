About Elegant Moments

A photography business in Anuradhapura, Sri Lanka, with a simple promise: capture your special moments.

Who we are

Two brands, one careful eye

Elegant Moments began as a photography studio in Anuradhapura, photographing families, birthdays, maternity, portraits and graduations with patience, play and a lot of love. Today it also includes Elegant Moments Weddings, for couples who want their story told beautifully, from the pre-shoot to the homecoming.

We don't just take photos. We follow the little looks, the nervous laughs and the happy tears, and turn them into a story you'll want to open again and again.

The way we work

The same four ideas shape every shoot, whether it is a first birthday or a wedding day.

I

Patience

II

Made to keep
